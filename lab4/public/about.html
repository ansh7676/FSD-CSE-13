Flow is a simple productivity app designed to help people focus on what matters, build meaningful habits, and make progress without the noise.

Our story

Productivity shouldn't feel like work.

We started Flow because modern productivity tools often made things more complicated instead of simpler.

Endless lists, notifications, dashboards, and systems can make it difficult to see the one thing that actually matters: what should you do next?

So we built Flow around a simple idea — give people enough structure to move forward, without creating another thing they have to manage.

Today, Flow brings planning, habits, focus, and progress together in one calm and focused experience.

What we believe

Simple principles.
Better products.

Everything we build starts with a few principles that keep us focused on creating a better experience.

01

Keep it simple

If a feature doesn't make someone's day easier, it probably doesn't belong in the product.

02

Respect attention

Your attention is valuable. Flow is designed to help you protect it instead of constantly demanding it.

03

Progress over perfection

Missing a day doesn't erase your progress. We believe consistency should feel encouraging, not punishing.

Our mission

Help people spend more time doing what matters.

We want productivity technology to feel less like another source of pressure and more like a quiet companion that helps you move through your day with intention.
